# Epic 32: Breeding Management System

## Epic ID: EPIC-32
## Epic Title: Advanced Breeding and Pedigree Management with TDD

### Vision Statement
Implement comprehensive breeding management with pedigree tracking, trait analysis, and automated nursery transplantation to support genetic improvement and breeding programs in agricultural operations.

### Business Objectives
- Track complete pedigrees across generations
- Analyze traits and genetic characteristics
- Automate nursery transplantation processes
- Implement trait comparison wizards
- Support breeding program management

### Success Criteria
- Complete pedigree tracking for breeding animals/plants
- Trait analysis with statistical reporting
- Automated nursery processes with efficiency improvements
- Trait comparison tools for breeding decisions
- Integration with genetic databases

### Acceptance Criteria
- Pedigree information is tracked across multiple generations
- Trait data is collected and analyzed with statistical tools
- Nursery transplantation processes are automated where possible
- Breeding decisions are supported by comparison tools
- Integration with genetic and performance databases works seamlessly

### Dependencies
- Livestock or plant management systems
- Genetic database integration
- Performance tracking systems

### Risks
- Data accuracy in pedigree tracking
- Complexity of genetic analysis algorithms
- Integration with external genetic databases

### Release Notes
- Phase 1: Basic pedigree tracking and trait collection
- Phase 2: Advanced analysis and statistical reporting
- Phase 3: Automated breeding decision support
