# Epic 44: Agricultural Processing Operations

## Epic ID: EPIC-44
## Epic Title: Agricultural Processing with By-Products and Recipe Management

### Vision Statement
Develop comprehensive agricultural processing capabilities that support by-product creation, custom recipe descriptions, workcenter energy cost tracking, and mass balance checks to optimize post-harvest operations and value-added processing.

### Business Objectives
- Enable complex processing operations with multiple outputs
- Support custom recipe management for specialized processing
- Track energy costs at workcenter level for efficiency optimization
- Implement mass balance checks for process accuracy
- Integrate with quality control systems

### Success Criteria
- Processing operations can generate multiple outputs including by-products
- Custom recipes can be defined with detailed processing instructions
- Workcenter energy costs are accurately tracked and allocated
- Mass balance checks ensure process accuracy and inventory control
- Integration with quality control for processed products

### Acceptance Criteria
- Processing orders can define multiple outputs including main products and by-products
- Recipe descriptions can include custom processing parameters and instructions
- Workcenter energy consumption is tracked and assigned to processing operations
- Mass balance checks prevent processing operations with quantity inconsistencies
- Process losses are tracked and reported for efficiency analysis

### Dependencies
- MRP (Manufacturing Resource Planning) system
- Quality control modules
- Inventory management system
- Workcenter and routing management

### Risks
- Complexity of multi-product processing operations
- Accurate energy cost allocation methodology
- Integration with existing inventory systems

### Release Notes
- Phase 1: Basic by-product support and recipe management
- Phase 2: Energy cost tracking and allocation
- Phase 3: Advanced mass balance and quality integration
