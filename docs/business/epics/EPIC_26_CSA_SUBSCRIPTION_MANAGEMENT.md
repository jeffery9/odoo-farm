# Epic 26: CSA Subscription Management

## Epic ID: EPIC-26
## Epic Title: Community Supported Agriculture Subscription Engine

### Vision Statement
Create an automated subscription management system for CSA programs that handles weekly vegetable bag subscriptions, automatic delivery generation, and member management to support local agricultural businesses and direct-to-consumer farming models.

### Business Objectives
- Streamline CSA subscription and billing processes
- Automate delivery schedule generation
- Support flexible subscription models and modifications
- Provide member management and communication tools
- Integrate with farm operations for harvest planning

### Success Criteria
- Automatic generation of delivery schedules based on subscription models
- Support for different bag types and customization options
- Integration with harvest planning and farm operations
- Member self-service capabilities for account management
- Automated billing and payment processing

### Acceptance Criteria
- Farmers can configure different CSA subscription models and bag types
- Subscription renewals and modifications are handled automatically
- Delivery schedules are generated based on subscription terms
- Members can manage their subscriptions through a portal
- Integration with accounting and payment systems works seamlessly

### Dependencies
- Farm operation management system
- Payment processing integration
- Harvest and yield tracking system
- Member management capabilities

### Risks
- Complexity of subscription term management
- Integration with diverse payment systems
- Seasonal variability and subscription modifications

### Release Notes
- Phase 1: Basic subscription management and delivery generation
- Phase 2: Member portal and self-service capabilities
- Phase 3: Advanced payment and billing integration
